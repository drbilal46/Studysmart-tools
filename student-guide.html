1. What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It represents the weighted average of grade points earned in a group of subjects. GPA is commonly calculated on a 4.0 scale, although grading systems may vary between institutions.

GPA = Total (Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours
How to use our tool: Enter the GPA and credit hours for each subject in the GPA Calculator and then select Calculate GPA.

2. What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

CGPA = Total (Semester GPA × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours

A weighted calculation is important when different semesters have different numbers of credit hours.

Enter the GPA and credit hours for each semester in the CGPA Calculator to calculate a weighted CGPA.

3. How to Calculate Percentage

Percentage shows the marks obtained compared with the total possible marks.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example

If a student obtains 450 marks out of 500:

(450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%
Enter your obtained marks and total marks in the Percentage Calculator to calculate the result quickly.

4. Aggregate Calculator

An aggregate combines different components using their assigned weightages. This can be useful when academic results depend on multiple components.

For example, different examinations or academic components may contribute different percentages to an overall aggregate.

Enter the obtained marks, total marks and weightage for each component. You can add multiple components and then calculate the overall aggregate.
Important: Always use the official weightage formula provided by your school, college, university or admission authority when an official aggregate is required.

5. What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measure based on weight and height.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI does not directly measure body fat and should be interpreted appropriately for the person's age, sex, and population.

Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimeters in the BMI Calculator to calculate BMI.

6. Healthy Weight Range

The Healthy Weight Range tool provides an approximate weight range based on height.

For adults, commonly used BMI-based calculations can be used as a general screening reference. Different populations and age groups may require different considerations.

This tool is for general educational information and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

7. Exam Marks and Grades

The Exam Marks Calculator can help calculate percentage from obtained and total marks.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

The appropriate grade depends on the grading policy of your school, college or university.

Important: Always check your institution's official grading scale because grade boundaries can differ between institutions.

8. Age Calculator

The Age Calculator calculates age using a person's date of birth.

Select the date of birth and use the Age Calculator to calculate the current age.

9. Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator helps calculate the discount amount and the final price after applying a percentage discount.

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

10. Simple Interest

Simple interest is calculated using the principal amount, interest rate and time.

Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100
Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

The rate is expressed as a percentage and the time is entered in years in our Simple Interest Calculator.

11. Unit Converter

Unit conversion changes a measurement from one unit to another equivalent unit.

Common examples

  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 kilometer = 1000 meters
  • 1 kilogram = 1000 grams
Use the Unit Converter to perform supported conversions quickly.
