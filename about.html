About StudySmart Tools

Welcome to StudySmart Tools, a free online platform created to provide simple and useful tools for students.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday academic calculations and study-related tasks easier, faster and accessible on mobile phones and computers.

Our Tools

StudySmart Tools provides calculators such as percentage, GPA, CGPA, age, BMI, discount, simple interest and other useful student tools.

For Students

Our tools are designed to be simple and easy to use for students around the world.
