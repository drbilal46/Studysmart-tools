What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It represents the average grade points a student earns in a group of subjects. GPA is commonly calculated on a 4.0 scale.

GPA = Total (Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours
Tip: Enter the GPA and credit hours for each subject in our GPA Calculator to calculate your weighted GPA.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents the overall academic performance of a student across multiple semesters.

CGPA = Total (Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours

Because credit hours can differ between semesters, a weighted calculation gives a more accurate overall result.

How to Calculate Percentage

Percentage shows how many marks a student obtained compared with the total possible marks.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example

If a student obtains 450 marks out of 500:

(450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measure based on a person's weight and height.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI categories can vary by age and population. For adults, commonly used ranges include underweight, normal weight, overweight and obesity.

How to Calculate Exam Grade

First calculate the percentage from the obtained marks and total marks. The grading scale can then be applied according to your institution's grading policy.

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100
Important: Different universities and schools may use different grade boundaries. Always check your institution's official grading policy.

Unit Conversion

Unit conversion changes a measurement from one unit into another equivalent unit.

Common examples

1 meter = 100 centimeters

1 kilogram ≈ 2.20462 pounds

1 kilogram = 1000 grams
