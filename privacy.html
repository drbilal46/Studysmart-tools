Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to StudySmart Tools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that may be provided when you use our website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and handled when you access or use StudySmart Tools.

Important: StudySmart Tools is designed primarily to provide free educational calculators, quizzes, study tools, and related resources. We do not require users to create an account to use our basic tools.

1. Information We Collect

Most StudySmart Tools features can be used without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

Depending on how you use the website, information may be provided voluntarily, such as your name, email address, or message when you use our Contact Us form.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our basic calculators and study tools.

2. Calculator Information

The website provides calculators such as GPA, CGPA, percentage, BMI, age, aggregate, discount, interest, unit conversion, and other educational tools.

Information entered into these calculators is used to perform the requested calculation. Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information into calculator fields.

3. Contact Us Information

If you contact StudySmart Tools, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, and message.

This information may be used only for purposes such as responding to your question, handling feedback, addressing technical problems, considering suggestions, or responding to business and collaboration inquiries.

4. How We Use Information

Information that is voluntarily provided may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Review feedback and suggestions.
  • Investigate reported technical issues.
  • Improve website features and user experience.
  • Respond to collaboration or business inquiries.
  • Maintain and improve the security and functionality of the website.

5. Cookies

StudySmart Tools may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand general website usage, or support future advertising and analytics services.

Cookies may be provided by StudySmart Tools or by third-party services used on the website.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics services in the future to understand general website traffic and how visitors interact with StudySmart Tools.

Analytics providers may collect information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and general usage information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising

StudySmart Tools may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements.

Third-party advertising services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use or link to third-party services, websites, tools, analytics providers, advertising providers, or other external services.

StudySmart Tools does not control the privacy practices of third-party services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information submitted through our website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

StudySmart Tools provides general educational resources and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional personal information when using our website. You may also manage cookies through the settings of your web browser, although disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

12. Data Retention

Information provided through communication channels may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide support, maintain records, or meet applicable legal and operational requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
